## The steps

Everything is self-serve at [dash.aifinpay.io](https://dash.aifinpay.io):

1. Create an account and a site.
2. Give it a payout address (a standard `0x…` EVM address). A new site
   accepts every network AiFinPay has switched on for that address; change
   the list under **Accepted networks**.
3. Press **Connect your site** (or **Add gateway** on **Site settings**): pick
   a slug, enter your upstream URL and leave **Paid address on** ticked. Your
   paid address is `https://gateway.aifinpay.io/{your-slug}/…` — that is the
   URL you hand to agents. It starts forwarding once you add the TXT record
   `_aifp-verify.<upstream-host>` that the **Prove the site is yours** card
   shows. Until then it answers `403 gateway_unavailable`.
4. **Close free access** so agents cannot skip the paid address. **Read
   [Hosted Gateway](/charge/gateway) before you do — the rule differs for an API
   and for a site with human readers, and using the API rule on a website will
   403 your readers and drop you out of search.**
5. Prove your own domain: set **Site URL** under **Profile & keys**, then add
   the TXT record that the **Prove the site is yours** card on the account
   **Dashboard** shows for it. Agents using the [AiFinPay MCP server](/pay/mcp)
   do not pay until the domain is proven.

[Hosted Gateway](/charge/gateway) walks through these steps with no code at all;
[self-hosted verification](/charge/dashboard) is the alternative if you would
rather keep traffic on your own domain and verify receipts yourself.
