This page is the public economics source of truth. If another page, SDK, bridge
or contract disagrees with it, that component is not release-compatible.

## Canonical route models

| Route | Payer total | Merchant/provider | AiFinPay | Creator/referral | Network gas |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **AIFP-1** | exact quoted gross | 99% | 1% | 0% | separate |
| **AIFP-2 / x402** | provider price | 100% | 0% | 0% | separate |

AIFP-1 is **gross-inclusive**. The protocol fee is deducted from the quoted
gross amount; it is never added on top. AIFP-2/x402 has a temporary 0% AiFinPay
protocol fee for the current launch period. No expiry is encoded yet, so a later
change requires a versioned economic decision and coordinated backend/SDK/docs
release.

## AIFP-1 reference tiers

One billing unit is **$0.0005**.

| Tier | Billing units per call | Gross price per call |
|---|---:|---:|
| `standard` | 1 | $0.0005 |
| `complex` | 4 | $0.002 |
| `premium` | 10 | $0.005 |

## Exact 6-decimal vectors

Stablecoin math is integer math. The current regression vectors are:

| Gross units | Merchant | AiFinPay | Creator | Conservation |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 500 | 495 | 5 | 0 | 500 |
| 2,000 | 1,980 | 20 | 0 | 2,000 |
| 5,000 | 4,950 | 50 | 0 | 5,000 |

The protocol fee is `floor(gross × 100 / 10,000)`; merchant receives the
remainder and creator receives zero. An amount too small to produce a non-zero
AIFP-1 protocol fee must be rejected rather than silently changing economics.

## Batches and receipts

A quote states the gross batch amount. One on-chain settlement purchases the
batch; a signed bearer receipt meters billing units locally at the merchant.
Receipt scope and quota do not change the economic split.

The reference minimum batch is $0.10:

| Tier | Minimum whole calls |
|---|---:|
| `standard` | 200 |
| `complex` | 50 |
| `premium` | 20 |

## Limits

Per-payment and daily operator caps are checked before signing. A model/tool
argument may tighten an operator ceiling but must not increase it. Unknown asset
price, invalid amount, expired quote or split mismatch is a blocking state.

## Production gate

A listed network or source implementation is not automatically payment-live.
Before signing, the release must independently pin and verify:

1. route class and chain ID;
2. B2BSplitter v1.3 address and exact runtime code hash;
3. on-chain BPS profile (AIFP-1 `100/0`, AIFP-2 `0/0`);
4. merchant/provider target and asset metadata;
5. quote expiry and exact gross calldata;
6. paid end-to-end evidence for that release;
7. no unresolved Critical/High fund-loss audit issue.

Until every item passes, settlement stays disabled/fail-closed.
